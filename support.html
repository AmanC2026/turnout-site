Support

A real person reads this. Email pckpapp8@gmail.com and say what happened.

Someone behaved badly

Tap their name in the game or group and choose Report or block. The report goes to an admin; they are never told who reported them. Blocking hides you from each other immediately, and they are not told that either.

If someone is in danger right now, call 911. A report reaches a person who reads it when they can — it is not an emergency service.

Delete my account

Settings → Delete my account. It removes your profile, sports, availability, photo and messages. Games you host are handed to another player where possible, or cancelled.

A court is wrong or missing

Court information comes from OpenStreetMap, so some of it is out of date. Email us and we'll fix it.

A game or group problem

Hosts can edit, cancel, and remove players from their own games. Group owners can do the same for their group. If something is stuck, email us.

Privacy questions

See the privacy policy — particularly the part about location, which is the question people most often have. Short answer: your position is blurred to about 500 metres before it is ever stored.
