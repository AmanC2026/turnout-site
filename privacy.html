Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

This app helps you find people nearby who want to play a sport. That needs some information about you. This page says exactly what it keeps, what it does not, and why — in plain words rather than legal padding.

The short version. We never store where you are. When you say you're free to play, your position is blurred to roughly 500 metres before it is written down, and distances are rounded to a tenth of a mile before they leave our server. The app can tell another player you are "about two miles away" and that is genuinely all it knows.

Location, in detail

Your device gives the app your position only when you have granted permission, and only while the app is open. We never track you in the background.

Two things then happen before anyone else could ever see it:

  • If you switch on "up for a game", the position is snapped to a grid of about 500 metres by the database itself, before the row is saved. Your exact position is never written anywhere.
  • Distances shown to other players are rounded to a tenth of a mile on our server, so even the number sent to another phone is coarse.

This is deliberate. Data that is never recorded cannot be leaked, stolen, or demanded.

One thing stays on your phone only: the name of the area the app last showed you (for example "Seminole Heights"), so the home screen can open instantly next time. It is never sent to our servers, and reinstalling the app clears it.

What we do not collect

  • Your home address, or any precise location, ever
  • Your phone number
  • Your contacts, photos library, calendar, or microphone
  • Payment details (the app is free and takes no payments)
  • Advertising identifiers. There is no advertising in this app and we do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

Who else sees your information

  • Other players. Your name, photo, bio, area, sports and levels are visible to signed-in players. Games you join show your name to others in that game. Messages are visible to that game or group.
  • Supabase, which hosts our database, sign-in and photo storage on our behalf. They process data under our instructions and do not use it for their own purposes.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., San Francisco), our crash-reporting service. It receives the technical crash reports described above — phone model, OS version, what the app was doing — and a random account identifier so we can see whether one person hits the same crash twice. It never receives your name, email, messages or location.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell personal information, and we have no advertising partners.
  • We may disclose information if we are legally required to, or where it is genuinely necessary to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep it

Your account and its content stay until you delete them. "Up for a game" statuses expire by themselves within hours. Reports and safety records are kept longer, because deleting them would make repeat problems invisible.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete my account, inside the app. It removes your profile, sports, availability, photo and messages. Games you host are handed to another player where possible, or cancelled, so other people aren't left standing on a court. This cannot be undone.

Age

This app is for people aged 16 and over. We check your date of birth at sign-up and the check is enforced by the database, not just the form. Where your app store provides an age category for your account, we rely on that too, and it takes precedence over the date you typed. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

Why we are allowed to use it

We use your information because it is needed to provide the service you signed up for (your account, games, groups and messages), because you chose to give it (your location while you are "up for a game", your photo and bio), and, for security logs and safety records, because we have a legitimate interest in keeping the service safe for everyone. If you are in the UK or European Union, those are the legal bases we rely on, and you can contact us to exercise any of the rights below.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit or remove most of your information from inside the app at any time.
  • Turn location permission off in your phone's settings. The app still works; it shows the Tampa Bay area instead of what's near you.
  • Delete your account entirely, as above.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your information, and to object to certain processing. Email us and we will help.

Security

Access rules are enforced inside the database rather than only in the app, so a rule cannot be bypassed by talking to our servers directly. Passwords are hashed by our sign-in provider. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell affected users promptly if something goes wrong.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app.
