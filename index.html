Keep the people worth playing with.

Most sports apps help you book a court. The hard part was never the court — it's getting the same eight people back next Tuesday.

How it works

  • See what's on. 1,238 courts, pitches and tracks around Tampa Bay are already in the app, with whatever games people have posted at them.
  • Or say you're free. Switch your status on for a few hours and nearby players see that you're up for something.
  • Turn the good ones into regulars. Make a group, and your people get first refusal on the spots — every week, without anyone having to organise it.

About your location

Your position is blurred to about 500 metres before it is stored, and distances are rounded to a tenth of a mile on the server. Another player can see that you're "about two miles away" and nothing more precise than that — not because the screen hides it, but because nothing sharper exists. Full detail in the privacy policy.

Where it is

In development, being tested in Tampa Bay. Not yet on the App Store or Google Play.
